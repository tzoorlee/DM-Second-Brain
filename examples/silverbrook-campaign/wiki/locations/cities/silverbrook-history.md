# Chronology of Silverbrook

> Append-only. Each entry dated in the in-world calendar.

## [1492-Kythorn-10 | session-01]
- The party arrived on a rainy evening and lodged at [[the-mended-kettle|The Mended Kettle]].
- [[elder-maren|Elder Maren]] hired them to investigate livestock disappearances near the eastern hills.

## [1492-Kythorn-12 | session-02]
- The party cleared the [[sunken-crypt|Sunken Crypt]]; the livestock raids stopped.
- A [[tarnished-silver-locket|Tarnished Silver Locket]] was recovered; the broken crown symbol unsettled Maren.
