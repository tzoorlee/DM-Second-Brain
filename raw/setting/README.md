# Setting — Reference Material

> Reference material the agent reads as a SOURCE, but that is not canon by itself:
>
> - notes on a **published setting** you play in (Forgotten Realms, Eberron, an adventure
>   module): what you use, summaries, excerpts from books you own;
> - the **history of another campaign** whose world this campaign inherits, copied by the
>   companion app into `from-<campaign-id>/`.
>
> What becomes canon is what you approve: the world-bible's `## Setting` (with its
> **Deviations**, which win over published lore), `raw/worldbuilding/` and the sessions.
> Lore the agent knows from outside `raw/` is never canon.

## Keep it private

Excerpts from published books are copyrighted. They are fine in your own private vault;
never publish or share a vault that holds them.
